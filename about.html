About me

I’m Thomas Pugh, an InterMaths student studying interdisciplinary mathematics at the University of L’Aquila.

I plan to continue my master’s studies in Vienna from March 2027, finishing in 2028. I’m interested in numerical methods for PDEs, simulation, and how mathematical ideas become useful computations. I’m also exploring quantum computing.

Before starting InterMaths, I worked as a software engineer at First Databank, where I built and evaluated AI applications. I studied mathematics and computer science at Indiana University, with a minor in chemistry.

Outside my studies and software work, I enjoy electronic music production and learning languages.
